Privacy by design. Focus by choice.

WakeLock follows a local-first approach. Your productivity information is designed to remain on your device rather than being automatically uploaded to a cloud account.

01. Introduction

Welcome to WakeLock. WakeLock is a productivity and focus management application designed to help users build better habits, manage daily tasks, improve concentration and maintain a productive lifestyle.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information WakeLock accesses, how it is used, where it is stored and how it is protected.

02. Information We Access

WakeLock may access or store the following information to provide its features.

Personal Profile Information

  • Name provided during onboarding.
  • Personal productivity goals.
  • Experience level.
  • Daily available working time.
  • Preferred working hours.

Productivity Information

  • Daily missions and task progress.
  • Completed and pending tasks.
  • Habit records and completion history.
  • Productivity streaks and achievements.
  • Pomodoro focus session history.

Events and Reminders

  • Event titles and descriptions.
  • Event dates and times.
  • Recurrence and reminder preferences.
  • Event completion status.

Application Selection Information

If you use Strict Mode, WakeLock may access installed, launchable application information to display applications for selection.

  • Application names.
  • Application package identifiers.
  • Your selected application preferences.

03. How We Use Information

Information accessed by WakeLock is used to:

  • Personalize your productivity experience.
  • Recommend daily missions based on your goals.
  • Track habits, progress and achievements.
  • Manage focus sessions.
  • Schedule events and reminders.
  • Provide Strict Mode configuration.
  • Save your preferences and restore progress.

We do not sell your personal information.

04. Data Storage

WakeLock is designed with a local-first approach. Information generated and entered within the application is stored locally on your device using its local database.

The current version does not require:

  • User account registration.
  • A cloud account.
  • A remote application backend.
  • Firebase authentication or cloud storage.

Your productivity information is not automatically uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling the application or clearing its application data may permanently remove locally stored information.

05. Permissions

WakeLock may request certain Android permissions to provide its functionality.

Notifications

Used to deliver reminders and productivity-related notifications.

Exact Alarm Permission

Used to schedule reminders and notifications at user-selected times.

Installed Application Visibility

Used to display installed applications when configuring Strict Mode.

Accessibility Permission

If Strict Mode app-blocking functionality is enabled, Android Accessibility Service permission may be requested.

This permission is intended to identify the currently foreground application and support configured focus restrictions, with the user's explicit consent.

WakeLock does not intend to collect passwords, read personal messages, record keystrokes or capture screen contents through this functionality.

Users remain in control of Android accessibility permissions and can disable the service through Android settings. Essential system functions and emergency calling must remain accessible.

06. Data Sharing

WakeLock does not sell, rent or trade personal information.

The current local-only version does not transmit productivity information to third-party advertising or analytics services.

Information stored locally may be accessible to the device owner and to anyone who has access to the unlocked device.

07. Data Retention and Deletion

Your information remains stored on your device until you delete it or remove the application's stored data.

You can remove locally stored information by:

  • Using available deletion options within the application.
  • Clearing WakeLock's application storage through Android settings.
  • Uninstalling WakeLock.

Deletion of application data may result in permanent loss of your productivity history and settings.

08. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored within the application. However, no electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

We recommend keeping your device updated, using a secure screen lock and protecting access to your device.

09. Children's Privacy

WakeLock is intended for general productivity and personal organization.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through online services. The current version does not provide an online account registration or cloud data collection service.

Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of mobile applications.

10. Third-Party Services

WakeLock may rely on Android operating system functionality for notifications, alarms, installed application visibility and accessibility features.

These platform services are governed by their respective providers' privacy policies and terms.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when WakeLock introduces new features, changes its data practices or when legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published with a revised Last Updated date. Users are encouraged to review this policy periodically.

12. Your Privacy Rights

You can:

  • Choose what personal information you enter into WakeLock.
  • Manage application permissions through Android settings.
  • Configure or disable supported features.
  • Delete locally stored application data.
